THE ORIGIN OF SWEETVILLE

Before there were streets, there was a melody.

Sweetville was born the moment Jahntella imagined a place where every song could become somewhere real. A chorus could become a glowing district. A lyric could become a hidden path. A feeling could become a home.

“Sweetville is the place where every Jahntella song exists.”

The first lights appeared at Melody Studio. Pink Café followed soon after. Then came Sparkle Lake, Donut District, and Neon Sweetheart. Each location carries a different piece of Jahntella’s heart.

THE SIX RULES OF SWEETVILLE

The world is held together by kindness.

01No one is judged here.

02Kindness is cool.

03Music is everywhere.

04Dreams have addresses.

05Joy matters.

06Every Sweetie belongs.
